# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view room availability for a time slot, so that I can choose a free room for study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Availability shows rooms that are not blocked and have no overlapping booking.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book a free room for a future time slot, so that I can reserve a place for individual or group study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A time slot of exactly two hours is permitted.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel a booking I made, so that the room becomes available when I no longer need it.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A student may cancel only their own booking.

### US-04
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a room, so that unusable rooms are unavailable for reservation and can return to service.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A blocked room remains visible in availability with its blocked status.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a period, so that I can understand how study rooms are being used.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** The administrator provides a start date and an end date for the period.

### US-06
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive confirmation after I book or cancel a room, so that I know whether my reservation changed.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Confirmation is sent only after a booking or cancellation succeeds.

### US-07
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to receive confirmation after a room is blocked or unblocked, so that I know the room status changed.
**Priority:** Low
**Assumption:** Confirmation of a room status change is available within the campus web application.
